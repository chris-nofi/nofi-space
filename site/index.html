DATA, EXPLAINED

Different sensors. Different questions.

Satellite data supports decisions; it does not measure every field condition or replace ground truth.

NASA · ECOSTRESS

Evapotranspiration

Thermal observations help estimate water loss from land and vegetation; coverage can be irregular.

NASA/USGS · HLS

Vegetation cover

Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel imagery helps track vegetation signals and NDVI.

NASA · POWER

Climate context

Weather and reference evapotranspiration provide broader climate context, not field-scale measurements.

NASA · SMAP

Soil moisture

Regional soil moisture observations are useful but much coarser than individual farm plots.

ESA · Sentinel-2

Crop cover detail

Partner imagery helps assess field vegetation, subject to cloud and pixel checks.

NASA · SRTM

Terrain and slope

Elevation and slope support relative erosion-risk screening.

ISRIC · SoilGrids

Soil properties

Modeled soil layers add context; local sampling remains important.

CHIRPS / GPM IMERG

Rainfall signals

Sources and substitutes are disclosed in each evidence file, including pending access.
