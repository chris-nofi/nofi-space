How it works

Four steps, from satellite to a decision you can check.

Pick a field

Open the map and choose one of the pilot fields: an olive grove on the Ilo coast, alfalfa in Samegua, potato in the Carumas highlands.

Read its score

The Ceres Field Score combines water, vegetation, soil, climate and erosion from named NASA and partner datasets, with its uncertainty shown.

Compare options

See four rotations or management options, including what you do today, re-rank them by your own priorities, and see how each held up over 45 past seasons.

Ask CERES

Tap "Talk to CERES" and ask out loud, in Spanish or English. It answers from the same NASA data and reminds you to check with the Agencia Agraria or INIA.

NASA data, explained

What each dataset measures and how the score uses it.

ECOSTRESS 70 m

Actual evapotranspiration (ET) from the space station. Divided by reference ET₀ it gives the water-stress part of the score.

NASA POWER 0.5°

Daily weather since 1981. Feeds FAO-56 reference ET₀, frost climatology and the 2001–2025 water model behind each strategy.

HLS (Landsat + Sentinel) 30 m

Harmonized surface reflectance for NDVI greenness at the field pixel, cross-checked with ESA Sentinel-2 at 10 m.

SMAP 9 km

Soil moisture against its 2015–2025 baseline. Used for Carumas today; a regional signal, not a field reading.

SRTM 30 m

Elevation and slope around each field. Combined with heavy-rain intensity it gives a relative erosion proxy, not soil loss.

MODIS · CHIRPS · ERA5-Land 250 m · 5 km · 9 km

Long NDVI history, cross-check rainfall (CHIRPS, alongside GPM IMERG) and frost series where POWER's grid cell is too high.

Satellites see a 30 m to 9 km pixel; a chacra is 1–2 ha. Ceres shows its gaps and confidence on every score. It supports, and does not replace, the farmer's judgment. Soil texture and carbon come from ISRIC SoilGrids, which is not a NASA product.

Verifiable by design · Avalanche

Every score points to evidence nobody can quietly change.

Farmers see a simple "registro verificable" badge. Underneath, the evidence file is hashed and the fingerprint is written to a WaterProofRegistry contract on Avalanche's Fuji C-Chain testnet.

x402 field API · for builders and judges

Pay-per-query NASA farm evidence.
